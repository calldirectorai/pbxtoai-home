Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Legacy Phone System Review?

It is a practical review of your phone bill, calling setup, phone numbers, routing, and business needs. The goal is to identify outdated services, possible cost issues, call-handling gaps, and sensible modernization options.

Can you tell if we still use POTS or analog lines just from our phone number?

No. A business phone number alone does not reliably reveal the underlying service. We evaluate your actual billing, equipment, carrier details, and call setup.

Do we have to replace our entire phone system?

Not necessarily. The right path depends on your current setup, business needs, devices, budget, and continuity requirements. We recommend changes only where they create a practical benefit.

Can we keep our current business phone number?

In many cases, yes. Number portability depends on the current provider, service type, and location, so we confirm that as part of the review and migration planning process.

What is SIP?

SIP is a technology used to deliver and manage voice communications over IP networks. In practical terms, it can help businesses modernize calling, improve flexibility, and reduce dependence on older phone infrastructure.

What is a cloud PBX?

A cloud PBX is a business phone system hosted and managed through the cloud rather than relying solely on a physical phone system at your location.

Is Voice AI right for every business?

No. Voice AI is most useful when a business has valuable inbound calls, repetitive questions, scheduling or routing needs, after-hours gaps, or missed-call issues. We recommend it only when it supports a clear business outcome.

What about alarm, elevator, security, fire, and other critical lines?

These devices require individual assessment. They may have specific connectivity, power, monitoring, equipment, code, or specialist requirements. PBXtoAI does not assume generic internet calling or AI calling is appropriate for those uses.
